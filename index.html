How these values are measured

Approximate movement sensing

These readings are approximate movement indicators, not medical measurements. They are derived from the Zippy arm’s movement-sensing inputs and finger movement patterns.

Forearm IR sensorTracks movement changes in the forearm area and contributes to the movement, smoothness, and overall movement estimates.
Two shoulder IR sensorsMonitor forward extension and sideways movement of the arm. Their combined readings contribute to the movement and range estimates.
Finger movementFinger closing and opening patterns contribute to finger closure, finger control, repetitions, and movement-cycle estimates.
Muscle stiffness estimateUses the movement pattern together with changes in detected motion consistency to produce an approximate stiffness indicator.
Summary valuesActive movement, finger repetitions, recovery balance, IR signal quality, and the Today’s interpretation section are calculated from the movement data shown on this page rather than entered separately.
